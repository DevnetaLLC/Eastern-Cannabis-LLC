# What Does This Mean

During the start of Eastern Cannabis LLC, we were working in the Central portion of New York State (between Albany, NY and Rochester, NY) cultivating and processing recreational and medical cannabis and cannabis-related products. **This is no longer the case**. <br />

After halting operations in early 2024, we begun the spin down of the company for eventual closing of cannabis operations. As of December 15, 2025 at 5:30 PM, we ended all contracts, relations, and business dealings with anything related to the cultivation or processing of medical and recreational cannabis and cannabis-related materials. <br />

We do extend a very warm thank you to our community for giving us the support, drive, and patience necessary for us to provide for you and on our exit from the industry. <br />

Though we no longer have plans to shutdown Eastern Cannabis LLC, just plan to re-franchise ourselves to what we are more dedicated to working with and familiar with. With a deep understanding of computer architecture, software engineering, analytics, and the overall technical space we shift into it with our future endeavors at Eastern Cannabis LLC. <br /> 


While we no longer offer individuals and businesses with high-quality cannabis products, we do still deliver you the highest quality service, experience and customer support and hope to see you in the future for you technical needs. <br />
